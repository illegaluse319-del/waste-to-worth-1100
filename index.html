01 — The initiative

A classroom that refused to throw things away.

As part of the ESG Theme 2026–27, the Department of Commerce & Management Science gathered more than twenty-five student volunteers. One hundred and fifty plastic bottles became outdoor trash cans. Two hundred bottle caps became a Brand Management Tree — a teaching tool built from waste.

Principal Dr. Mazahar Ahmed Farooqui thanked the students and reminded the campus that turning waste into value is a habit of responsible choice. Maulana Azad College continues to be recognised as a Green Champion; this campaign is another proof of that promise.

Turning waste into value inspires responsible choices in life.

Dr. Mazahar Ahmed Farooqui, Principal

02 — From waste, to worth

Bottles became bins. Caps became a tree. The lesson stayed.

Eco bins from 150 bottles

Empty PET bottles were washed, stacked and bound into working dustbins for the campus — labelled Clean Campus, Green Campus.

A Brand Management Tree

Two hundred caps mapped identity, architecture, trust and image — a commerce classroom built from what would have been landfill.
